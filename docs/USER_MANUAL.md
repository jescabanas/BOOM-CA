# User Manual

## For Barangay Secretaries

1. **Sign in** at the platform URL with the email/password provided by your
   DILG Super Administrator.
2. Your **My Submissions** page lists every open mandate and its deadline.
3. Click **Upload MOV** next to a mandate, choose your file (PDF, DOCX, XLSX,
   ZIP, image, or video), and confirm. The system timestamps the upload the
   moment it lands — that timestamp is what determines your score, so
   don't wait until the last minute.
4. Status column shows: *Not submitted → Under review → Approved / Rejected /
   Correction requested*. If a submission is rejected or sent back for
   correction, the reviewer's remarks explain why — you can re-upload as
   long as the submission isn't locked.
5. Your score for each mandate appears once an evaluator reviews it (or
   automatically once the deadline passes, if nothing was submitted).

## Reviewing submissions (Super Administrators)

1. Sign in, then select **Review** in the sidebar.
2. Filter by status (Under review, Pending, Approved, Rejected, All).
3. Click **Review** on a submission to see attached documents.
4. Choose **Approve**, **Reject**, or **Request Correction**. Rejections and
   correction requests require remarks — the barangay secretary sees these
   immediately as a notification.
5. Once approved, the score already calculated by the system (5 / 3 / 0
   based on timing) stands as the barangay's official score for that
   mandate.

## Analytics and reporting (Super Administrators)

1. Sign in → **City Dashboard**: summary cards (total barangays, compliant,
   pending, late, rejected, total documents, average score), submission
   trend chart, compliance donut, mandate completion bar chart, and a live
   top-performers table.
2. Use **Export CSV / Excel / PDF** on the top-performers table for reports.
3. Visit **Leaderboard** for the full, filterable ranking of all barangays.

## For the Public

- No login required. Visit the **Public Leaderboard** page directly.
- Filter by cluster. Rankings update roughly hourly.
- This is the same underlying data DILG staff see — there's no separate
  "public" score, just a read-only view of the same computed rankings.

## Notifications

All roles see in-app notifications for:
- Submission received / approved / rejected
- Correction requested
- Deadline approaching (48 hours out) / passed
- Evaluation completed

## Getting Help

Contact your Super Administrator for account issues (password reset, role
changes, disabled account). Technical issues with the platform itself should
go to your DILG IT support contact.
